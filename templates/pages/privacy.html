Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who I am

CJB Web Development is a UK based software development business run by Curtis Booth. This site is cjbwebdevelopment.com. I am the data controller for any personal data you provide through this site.

What I collect

When you use the contact form or the hire form, I collect the information you choose to give me: your name, email address, and the details of your enquiry. I do not collect any data beyond what you submit.

How I use it

I use your details only to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the work we agree on. I do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for it to function correctly. I do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Where a cookie is set, it is limited to the minimum needed for the site to work.

Third party services

Where I use a third party to process data on my behalf (for example, to deliver form submissions by email), that provider acts only on my instructions and is not permitted to use your data for its own purposes.

How long I keep data

I keep enquiry data only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow up. Once it is no longer needed, it is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete the personal data I hold about you at any time. To do so, contact me through the contact page and I will respond within one month.

Contact

For any privacy questions, use the contact form.
